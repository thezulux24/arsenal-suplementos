## Scope

Storefront home (`/`), catalog, product page, cart drawer, checkout, order and tracking pages of Arsenal Suplementos. Home is Persuade; catalog, cart and checkout are Operate inside the same world.

## Audience and job

Cali buyers who train and care for everyday wellbeing; they choose by goal, check price and INVIMA registration, add to cart, pay with Wompi or ask on WhatsApp.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shop is an azulejo station hall: every goal is a framed tile panel and every product is painted onto its own tiles, with the grout seams running straight through. It refuses the pharmacy-chain banner, icon-category row and product carousel.

OWN-WORLD: Cobalt deep #1B3A8F, cobalt medium #3B63C4, cobalt wash #DCE5F6 on milk white #F7F6F1; mustard #D9A21B only inside border frames. Hairline grout grid, double-rule cartouche frames with small fleuron corners, geometric padrão friezes between panels, roman capitals (Marcellus) for captions and names, Hanken Grotesk for body and prices. Buttons are glazed plaques: cobalt fill with inner rule. Packshots keep their own colour on white tiles.

STORY: The visitor sees the hall's name cartouche, understands it is a Cali supplement shop with real brands and prices, picks a goal panel, adds to cart from the tile, and pays or writes on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width tile wall framed by a frieze: a cartouche spanning six tiles carries the h1 and the hard fact (shipping from Cali, registration on every product); a 3x3 tile panel holds the featured product with price and a working Agregar plaque; a column of single tiles shows three more products, each addable. Primary action: Agregar al carrito on the featured panel.

FORM: Azulejo station hall, the user's adopted challenger (catalog id architecture-places-azulejo-station-hall); code-led. seed: 63b85a731a39e74a

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction and motion

Cobalt glaze wash: on hover or selection a tile's line work fills with cobalt like glaze in the kiln (background-size wash, 320 ms ease-out); the active goal panel deepens while neighbours fade toward glaze white. Adding to cart fires one wash across the cart plaque. No scroll-linked motion.

## Constraints

Every word, price, image and toggle comes from the CMS (`arsenal`). No invented reviews, ratings or claims. Supplement legal line on every product page.
